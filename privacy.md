# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord application (the "App") handles information when you use it.

## Information the App May Process

To provide its features, the App may access or store:

- Discord IDs for users, servers, channels, and messages.
- Usernames and display names.
- Commands you run and the inputs you provide to them.
- Server-specific settings configured by administrators.

The App does not intentionally collect passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate, maintain, and improve the App, such as responding to commands, saving settings, and preventing abuse. It is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

## Data Retention

Data is kept only as long as needed to provide the App's features. Server data may be deleted when the App is removed from a server.

## Your Choices

You can stop the App from processing new data by not using it or by removing it from your server. To request deletion of stored data, open an issue at [github.com/crizco23/discord-bot-legal](https://github.com/crizco23/discord-bot-legal/issues).

## Third Parties

The App operates on Discord, and your use of Discord is governed by the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
